How to Order 📝

Four cozy little steps and your cutie is on its way!

1

🧺 Fill your basket

Browse the segments, tap your favourite colour on each card and press Add to Cart.

2

📝 Place your order

Open the cart, press Place Order and fill in the order form with your details & address.

3

💜 Send bKash delivery fee

Send only the delivery charge via bKash to 01533964171, then enter the TrxID + your bKash number in the form. Orders without bKash proof cannot be placed!
